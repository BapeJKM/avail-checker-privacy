Privacy policy: Avail Checker

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Avail Checker is a browser extension that shows how many places are free on a ticket page. This policy says what it does with data.

What it reads

On a ticket page of a supported shop, the extension reads the availability data that the page itself has already received (the seat map, the free places, the prices). It reads the address and title of the open tab to recognise the shop and the event.

What it keeps, and where

  • On your device only. The text you type in the Search field, your options (language, theme, price, seat rules) and a list of the pages the panel has read are kept in your browser's own storage. You can delete the list in the panel (More, "Saved pages") at any time, and removing the extension removes everything.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The extension has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. It does not sell or share data.

What it asks the network for

Only the ticket shop you are looking at. To refresh a reading, the extension asks the shop's own address that the page already used, with your normal browser session. If you turn on the option for the size of the hall, it may also read a public venue file of the same ticket company. It does not contact any other server.

What it does not do

It does not buy tickets, add anything to a basket, sign in for you, read your passwords, payment details or messages, or change the pages you visit.

Permissions

The permissions are listed with the reason for each in the Chrome Web Store listing. Access to a shop that is not on the supported list is asked only when you add that shop, and only for that one site.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in the GitHub repository that hosts this page.

Changes
